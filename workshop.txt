description=[h2]Compatibility & Requirements[/h2]
description=Build 42; package minimum 42.20.3. No required mod dependencies. Powered pumps,
description=purification and heating optionally use B47ANCEDPower with legacy powerWorks
description=fallback. The sediment housing needs no electricity. Update enabled-mod lists
description=from waterWorks to B47ANCEDWaterworks; never enable both copies together.

description=[h2]Configuration[/h2]
description=Server sandbox settings control infrastructure actions, inspection, sampling and
description=sediment filtering. Sediment defaults: 100 water units per cartridge and 75%
description=particulate/turbidity removal per batch. Biological, chemical and salt hazards
description=remain unchanged. Cartridge replacement discards the old media.
