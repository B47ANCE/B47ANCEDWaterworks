[h1]B47ANCE’d Waterworks[/h1]

Water storage, plumbing, pumps, pressure, treatment, and distribution infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

B47ANCE’d Waterworks extends vanilla fluid containers through authoritative transfers, persistent quality, reusable networks, and safe provider APIs while preserving native storage behavior.

[b]Mod Version: 0.35.2[/b]

[b]Development Status[/b]

The fluid, quality, transaction, and network foundations are implemented; complete player-facing infrastructure and multiplayer validation remain in development.

MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. Native crafting and SP NOT TESTED; this is development source with stable-release G8 BLOCKED.

[h2]Compatibility & Requirements[/h2]
Build 42; package minimum 42.20.3. No required mod dependencies. Powered pumps,
purification and heating optionally use B47ANCEDPower with legacy powerWorks
fallback. The sediment housing needs no electricity. Update enabled-mod lists
from waterWorks to B47ANCEDWaterworks; never enable both copies together.

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
Server sandbox settings control infrastructure actions, inspection, sampling and
sediment filtering. Sediment defaults: 100 water units per cartridge and 75%
particulate/turbidity removal per batch. Biological, chemical and salt hazards
remain unchanged. Cartridge replacement discards the old media.
