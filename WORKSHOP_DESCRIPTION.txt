[h1]B47ANCE’d Waterworks[/h1]

Water storage, plumbing, pumps, pressure, treatment, and distribution infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

B47ANCE’d Waterworks extends vanilla fluid containers through authoritative transfers, persistent quality, reusable networks, and safe provider APIs while preserving native storage behavior.

[b]Mod Version: 0.34.1[/b]

[b]Development Status[/b]

The fluid, quality, transaction, and network foundations are implemented; complete player-facing infrastructure and multiplayer validation remain in development.
